# Contributing to OpenArtifact

Development happens at https://github.com/openartifact/openartifact. Please open issues and pull requests there. The development setup, workflow and conventions are in its [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/openartifact/openartifact/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

For security problems do not open an issue; see our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

By contributing you agree that your contributions are licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Please follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
